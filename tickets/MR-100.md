### BA → client · 2026-10-27
Thanks for the brief. Before we write the spec, we need your decisions on these. Each
one changes what we build. Please answer under each question; "not sure yet" is a fine
answer, and we'll plan around it safely. Background for each is in
`intent/persona-conversation.md`.

**Q1. Her written style only, or her voice too?**
Should the persona match the way she wrote and spoke in words, or also sound like her?
Why it matters: cloning a voice is a separate system with its own consent and misuse risks.
> Answer:

**Q2. Who is "Mom" in the data?**
Today each memory just carries a typed name for the person. Is a persona one named
person per memorial, and who decides that "Mom" and "Mẹ" are the same person?
Why it matters: we may need a record for the person before anything else.
> Answer:

**Q3. Who may talk with the persona?**
Only the family member who built the memorial, or others they invite? Who can take
access away again?
Why it matters: today memories are private to one account, so sharing is new.
> Answer:

**Q4. Can grandchildren under 18 use it, and with whose consent?**
Why it matters: safety and consent rules for children.
> Answer:

**Q5. What should it say when the memories don't cover a question?**
Why it matters: without a fixed reply, the AI tends to invent an answer.
> Answer:

**Q6. What does "remember what we talked about last time" mean?**
The full chat or a summary? Per family member or shared? For how long, and can a family
member delete it? Should anything said in a chat ever become a memory?
Why it matters: privacy, deletion, and whether chats can change the record of her life.
> Answer:

**Q7. Does it ever speak as her ("I remember…") or claim to be her?**
Should it always be clear that it's an AI recreation?
Why it matters: honesty with grieving families.
> Answer:

**Q8. Everything the AI found, or only what the family confirmed?**
Today every item the AI extracted can be used unless someone rejected it. Should the
persona use only items a family member has confirmed?
Why it matters: there's no "confirmed" step today; adding one changes what it knows.
> Answer:

**Q9. When is the next demo, and what must it show?**
A working backend API, or a conversation people can click through? (App screens are out
of scope this round.)
Why it matters: it decides what we slice into the first tickets.
> Answer:

**Q10. How fast must a reply feel?**
The AI runs on our own machine, which is slow without a GPU.
Why it matters: it decides streaming, model size and hardware, without moving family
data to an outside AI service.
> Answer:

**Q11. How will families judge that it "feels like talking to Mom"?**
Why it matters: we need a measurable outcome before we can test it.
> Answer:
