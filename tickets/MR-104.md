## What to build
- Tables and migration: `conversation`, `conversationmessage`, `personaturn` (IDs and timings only, no text).
- `conversations.py` router:
  - `POST /persons/{id}/conversations` → `{conversation_id, notice}`; the first stored message is `PERSONA_NOTICE`.
  - `POST /conversations/{id}/messages` → `text/event-stream` with `token`, then `done {sources, fallback, notice}`, or `error {detail}` (generic).
  - `POST /conversations/{id}/end` → `ended`, messages deleted. Calls the summary hook from MR-106 if present (a no-op until then).
- The reply comes from `persona_graph.reply(...)` (MR-105). Until it lands, wire a stub that yields `PERSONA_NO_MEMORY` with `fallback: true`.
- Writes one `personaturn` row per turn.
