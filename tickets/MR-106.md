## What to build
- `conversationsummary` table and migration; unique (person_id, user_id).
- `app/services/conversation_summary.py`: on end, one model call with `SUMMARY_SYSTEM_PROMPT` (topics and what the member shared about themselves, not facts about her); no call when there were no turns (AC-9.5).
- Idle sweep ends open conversations after `CONVERSATION_IDLE_MINUTES` (30) and summarizes them.
- `GET` and `DELETE /persons/{id}/summary`, for the caller's own summary only.
- Delete triggers: deleting a linked memory, or rejecting or correcting one of its items, deletes that person's summaries.
- Fill the summary slot in the persona context (MR-105).

## Files
`app/models.py` (summary table only), new migration, `app/services/conversation_summary.py`, `app/core/prompt.py` (`SUMMARY_SYSTEM_PROMPT`), `app/api/routes/conversations.py` (end + summary endpoints), `app/api/routes/extracted_memories.py` and `memories.py` (delete triggers), tests.
