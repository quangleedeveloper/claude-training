### Dev → BA · 2026-10-29
**DEV-1. Memory title.** The model needs a title and an offer has none. An accepted offer gets "Offered: " plus the first 60 characters of its text; the owner can rename it. Proposed; another rule is fine.
**DEV-2. Cap on waiting offers.** At most 20 waiting offers per member and person (409 beyond that), so one member cannot flood the owner's list. Not in the spec; the number is mine.
**DEV-3. The sender is forgotten on accept.** Accepting deletes the offer (it became the memory), so the owner can no longer see who sent it. If that matters, a later ticket could store `offered_by` on the memory (new column).
**DEV-4. Revoked members.** Their waiting offers stay for the owner to decide; the spec says nothing. Deleting a person or the member's account removes them.
**DEV-5. Files outside the list.** `tests/utils/person.py` (`create_random_offer`) and the regenerated frontend client. The offer screens are MR-114 (blocked on Q12).
**DEV-6. Fixes after review.** A blank offer ("   ") is refused (422) and offer text is trimmed; the generated title is one line (newlines collapsed); the 20-offer cap locks the person's row so two offers at once cannot both pass it. Still untested under real concurrency: the accept lock and the cap lock. If the model is down when an accepted offer is extracted, the memory exists with no items (same as any new memory) and the offer is already gone.
