## What to build
- **Owner.** A "Share" action on the people page opening a dialog:
  - email field and a required tick "I confirm this person is 18 or older"; the Invite button stays disabled until it is ticked;
  - the same confirmation text for any email, known or not (no "no such account" message);
  - a members list (name and email) with a Revoke action behind a confirm that says their chat ends and their summary is deleted.
- **Member.** A "Shared with me" list (from `GET /persons/shared`, name only) with an empty state; each entry opens the chat from MR-110.
- A revoked member's list loses the person on the next load, and a chat left open shows the "access removed" message from AC-13.7.

## Notes
- Forms use `react-hook-form` + `zod` with the schema above the component; the tick is `z.literal(true)` to mirror the API's `Literal[True]`.
- Use the generated `PersonsService` member calls; show the API's one generic confirmation text verbatim.
- The invite dialog must not reveal whether the email matched an account. Known limit: the members list does (spec Areas of concern, MR-107 DEV-3); the Lead decides if that needs pending invites.
- The e2e creates the invited account through the existing `createUser` helper in `frontend/tests/utils/privateApi.ts`, then logs in as them.
