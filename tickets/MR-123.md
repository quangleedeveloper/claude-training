## What to build
- Tool `search_memories(person_id, query, limit?)`: only the person's **owner**. Members, outsiders and superusers who are neither get "Not enough permissions" and nothing is returned (spec concern 1, the safe default until the Lead decides).
- Uses `search_persona` and returns, per hit, the extracted item text, the memory title and the score, best first. Never the memory's full story, its files, its owner, or an unconfirmed, rejected or other-person item.
- `limit` between 1 and 20; missing means `RETRIEVAL_TOP_K`. Anything else is invalid input. `query` is 1 to 500 characters, like `SearchRequest`.
- A person with no confirmed items returns an empty list, not an error.
