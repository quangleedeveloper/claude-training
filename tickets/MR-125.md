## What to build
- `add_memory` accepts a direct-memory request, owner only, and only while `MCP_ALLOW_DIRECT_MEMORY` is on (default `false`); otherwise it is refused and nothing is stored.
- Two calls. The first returns a confirmation token with a preview (the text and the person's name) and stores nothing. The second, with the token, creates a `Memory` owned by the caller, linked to the person, and starts extraction in the background; its items start unconfirmed.
- The token is signed and short-lived (`MCP_CONFIRMATION_TTL_SECONDS`, default 300), bound to user, person and the text, and works once inside the process. An expired, reused, mismatched or malformed token stores nothing and returns a generic error.
- Members are refused, with or without a token.
- Settings `MCP_ALLOW_DIRECT_MEMORY` and `MCP_CONFIRMATION_TTL_SECONDS` in `Settings` and `.env.example`.
